Need to know

FAQ

Do I have to choose a track while registering?

No. Tracks are discovery filters. Choose a problem statement when your team is ready.

How many people can be on a team?

Teams must have four members. The backend enforces the maximum.

Can we change our problem statement?

Until the challenge is locked, the event team can allow changes. The exact lock window will be shown in the event rules.

What if I already have a team?

Ask your leader for the team code and use the Join a team section above.

Something not working?
